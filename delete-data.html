Request deletion of Ralph's Voice data

Ralph's Voice
Effective date: 1 August 2026

Ralph's Voice has no user accounts. Most personalised information is stored only on the device and can be removed by the carer. The online services do not intentionally retain Buddy conversations or Piper speech requests after responding.

Delete data stored on the device

A carer can remove a profile, card, image, Buddy memory or downloaded speech clips from the relevant Ralph's Voice settings. To remove all Ralph's Voice data from an Android device, use Android Settings > Apps > Ralph's Voice > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Deleting app data also removes locally stored profiles, cards, preferences and downloaded speech clips on that device.

Request deletion from the developer

To request deletion of technical data held by the developer, email j4cu8.a@gmail.com with the subject "Ralph's Voice data deletion request". Please include the app version and an approximate date and time of use or of any crash report. Do not include card text, Buddy messages, photos or other sensitive information in the email.

Because there are no user accounts or device identifiers, this information is needed to identify a report without collecting unnecessary data.

What is deleted or kept

  • Buddy conversation content and Piper speech request text are processed to answer a request and are not intentionally retained by the developer.
  • Optional technical crash reports are kept for up to 90 days for troubleshooting, then automatically deleted. Where a report can be identified from the details above, it can be deleted sooner on request.
  • Encrypted backups are created and kept only where the carer chooses to save or share them. To delete one, remove it from that chosen device or storage provider.
