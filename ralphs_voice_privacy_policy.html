Privacy Policy

Ralph's Voice
Effective date: 1 August 2026
Data controller: Jake Adams, United Kingdom
Contact: j4cu8.a@gmail.com

Ralph's Voice is an augmentative and alternative communication app for children, families, carers and people with additional communication needs. This policy explains what the app stores, when online features send information, and the choices available to carers.

At a glance

  • There are no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, user accounts or in-app purchases.
  • Most personalised content stays on the device.
  • Online Buddy and Piper speech features send only the text needed to provide that feature when they are used.
  • Technical crash reporting is off by default and can be switched on by a carer.
  • Manual backups are encrypted before they are shared or saved.

Information stored on the device

Ralph's Voice stores personalised app content in its private app storage. This can include profile names, age or support details entered by a carer, card text, categories, custom images, selected pages, voice preferences, Buddy memory that a carer chooses to save, usage preferences, downloaded speech clips and Health page selections. This information is used so the app can personalise communication and work quickly offline.

Health page content is for communication support only. It is not a diagnostic, monitoring or medical advice service.

When information is sent online

Buddy

When Buddy is used with an internet connection, the current conversation, recent messages in that conversation, and the limited context needed to make Buddy helpful are sent to ai.j4cu8.uk. That context can include the active profile age and any Buddy memory a carer has chosen to enable. The service forwards the request to a developer-operated Ollama model to generate a reply. Conversation content is processed to respond and is not intentionally saved as a chat history on the service.

Speech

When the Piper voice option is selected and a phrase is not already available locally, the phrase text and selected voice are sent to ai.j4cu8.uk so a developer-operated Piper service can generate audio. The resulting audio can then be cached on the device for faster repeat playback. Choosing a device voice or using prepared clips can reduce or avoid online speech requests.

Technical crash reports

Technical crash reporting is optional and off by default. If a carer turns it on, the app may send a technical report after a crash. Reports are designed to include only the report identifier, time, app version, build, platform, source, exception information and stack trace. The crash reporting feature is designed not to include profiles, card content, Buddy messages, voice phrases, photos or device identifiers. Reports are stored on the developer-managed service for up to 90 days for troubleshooting, then removed automatically.

Service and infrastructure providers

Online requests are protected in transit using HTTPS and pass through Cloudflare's network before reaching the developer-operated services. Cloudflare may process connection information such as IP address and technical request data to deliver and secure the connection under its own Privacy Policy. Ralph's Voice does not sell personal information or use it for behavioural advertising.

Backups

A carer can create an encrypted Ralph's Voice backup. It is protected with the carer PIN before the app opens the device share sheet, where the carer chooses whether to save it locally or send it to a cloud-storage provider. That chosen provider's privacy policy then applies. Android system backup may also operate if it is enabled by the device owner; it is controlled by the Android device and the account selected by the owner.

Permissions

The Android app requests internet and network-state access so online Buddy, online Piper speech, backups and updates can work. Selecting or sharing an image or backup uses Android's system picker or share sheet. The app does not request location, contacts, SMS, camera or microphone permission for its current Android release.

Children and carers

Ralph's Voice is designed to be used with parent, guardian or carer involvement where appropriate. Please avoid entering unnecessary identifying or sensitive information into Buddy conversations or phrases that will be spoken online. Carers can control profiles, Buddy memory, voice settings, crash reporting and backups from Settings.

Retention and deletion

Personalised app data remains on the device until a carer deletes it, restores another backup over it, clears app storage or uninstalls the app. Downloaded speech clips can be cleared in Settings. Buddy conversation content and Piper phrase text are not intentionally retained on the online service after a response is provided. Optional crash reports are deleted automatically after up to 90 days.

Because there are no user accounts, anonymous technical reports may not be identifiable as belonging to a particular person. For help with a data request or deletion request, email j4cu8.a@gmail.com with enough non-sensitive detail to identify the issue, such as the approximate report time and app version.

Security

The app uses Android private app storage for local content, protects manual backups with encryption, stores the carer PIN using the device's secure storage where available, and uses HTTPS for online services. No system can guarantee absolute security, so carers should also keep the device locked and updated.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data handling changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
